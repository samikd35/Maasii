# Payments happen outside the system

Guests pay in their own bank or telebirr app, or in cash, using the Payment Options the venue publishes. A waiter then records a Payment Confirmation against the bill. We never collect, hold or move money. Doing so would make us a payment service under National Bank of Ethiopia rules and would need a licensed partner. Integrating telebirr or Chapa so that payment status updates automatically is a later option, not a foundation.
