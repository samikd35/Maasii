# The brand owns the menu and the look; each venue owns its service

Our first client, Bilo's, is one brand with six locations. We split the model in two. The Brand holds the logo, colours and master menu. Each Venue holds its tables, staff, shifts, sold-out dishes and any price differences. An independent café is simply a brand with one venue. A single flat "restaurant" record would be simpler at first, but it would force chains to maintain a separate menu at every location and would be expensive to untangle once data exists.
